## Map data

The offline map includes country outlines derived from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), whose map data is public domain, and city and administrative-area data derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). GeoNames data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The app's generated map data is a filtered and transformed subset of these sources.
